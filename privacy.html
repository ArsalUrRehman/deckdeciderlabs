Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 18, 2026

Deck Decider Labs (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates the mobile game Snubb (the “App”). This policy explains what data the App collects, how it’s used, and who it’s shared with.

Data we collect directly

The App itself does not require an account and does not collect your name, email, or any personally identifying information. Gameplay data — your score, coins, cosmetics, streaks, and challenge progress — is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Data collected by third-party services

The App uses the following third-party services, which may collect data independently under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — shows optional rewarded video ads (e.g. “watch an ad to continue”). Because the App is suitable for a general audience including children, every ad request is tagged as child-directed: AdMob serves only non-personalized, contextual ads capped at a general-audience content rating, and does not use advertising ID or behavioral data for ad personalization. AdMob may still collect general device and IP information needed to serve and measure ads. See the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from AdMob.
  • Google Play Games Services — if you sign in, it links your score to your Google Play Games profile for leaderboards, and backs up your progress (coins, cosmetics, streak, achievements) to your Google account’s Saved Games so it can be restored on a new device. This is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Who can play

Snubb is suitable for a general audience, including children as young as 5. It requires no account to play, and never collects a player’s name, email, or any other personally identifying information, regardless of age.

Children’s privacy

Because the App is played by children, ads are configured as described above — non-personalized, contextual only, general-audience rated — and no behavioral advertising data is collected from any player. This is intended to comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play’s Families Policies.

Google Play Games Services sign-in is optional and is only used for leaderboards and progress backup — it is never required to play. Parents with questions about a child’s use of Google Play Games Services, or requests to review or delete associated data, can contact us using the details below and we will help direct that request to Google.

Data retention and deletion

Local gameplay data lives only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its storage. To manage or delete data collected by Google AdMob, or progress backed up via Google Play Games Services, use your Google Account settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
