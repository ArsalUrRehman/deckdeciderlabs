Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 18, 2026

Deck Decider Labs ("we", "us", "our") operates the mobile game Snubb (the "App"). This policy explains what data the App collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with.

Data we collect directly

The App itself does not require an account and does not collect your name, email, or any personally identifying information. Gameplay data — your score, coins, cosmetics, streaks, and challenge progress — is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Data collected by third-party services

The App uses the following third-party services, which may collect data independently under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — shows optional rewarded video ads (e.g. "watch an ad to continue"). AdMob may collect device identifiers, advertising ID, IP address, and general device information to serve and measure ads. See the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from AdMob.
  • Google Play Games Services — if you sign in, it links your score to your Google Play Games profile for leaderboards. This is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party service used by the App, please contact us and we'll work with that service to address it.

Data retention and deletion

Local gameplay data lives only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its storage. To manage or delete data collected by Google AdMob or Google Play Games Services, use your Google Account settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
